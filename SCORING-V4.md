# Project Tracker Scoring V4

Effective: 25 Aug 2026

## Overall score

- 75% Project Delivery
- 15% Founder Accountability
- 10% Work Consistency
- Final score is always between 0 and 100.

## Automatic project scope

Every current project receives an automatic scope/weight estimate. Staff do not choose their own score weight.

- Quick = 0.15x
- Small = 0.5x
- Medium = 1.5x
- Major = 3x
- Critical = 4x

The estimate uses four kinds of evidence:

1. Planned time: project creation/assignment date to due date.
2. Number of meaningful project tasks.
3. Project nature: business/system outcomes such as revenue, ROAS, implementation, rollout, automation, LMS, policy, system, campaign, launch, student/teacher targets, fees, cleanup, dashboard, tracker, or scoring work.
4. Actual completion time as supporting evidence. Very small jobs with no strong business/system signal can be reduced to Quick when completed within a few hours.

Keeping a small job open longer never makes it more valuable.

Staff-created projects are automatically capped at Medium (1.5x) unless the founder overrides the weight. Founder override options are Auto, Quick 0.15x, Small 0.5x, Medium 1.5x, Major 3x, or Critical 4x.

The Add Project screen shows the estimated scope and weight before the project is saved. The estimate can change automatically after meaningful tasks are added or real execution evidence becomes available.

## Delivery scoring

Meaningful projects earn delivery points according to weight and outcome.

- Completing on or before the due date earns the best project outcome.
- Late completion receives a lower outcome score.
- An unfinished project that passes its due date receives a negative late penalty.
- Bigger overdue projects create a stronger penalty than tiny work.
- Quick jobs cannot create a large overall-score jump.

## Founder Accountability

- Uses distinct report days in a rolling 14-day window.
- Full credit requires 12 report days.
- Repeated Generate Report clicks on the same day do not add marks.

## Work Consistency

Only meaningful evidence counts, including:

- progress movement of at least 5%,
- real completion,
- a genuine Blocked update with a blocker reason.

Renaming a project, editing its due date, moving Current/Future, or repeatedly changing values does not create meaningful-work credit.

## My Performance explanation

Every employee dashboard includes a clickable score explanation in My Performance. It shows:

- the overall 75/15/10 formula,
- Founder Accountability earned/missing points,
- Work Consistency earned/missing points,
- every score-active project,
- automatic scope and weight,
- project outcome score,
- positive points earned,
- negative late penalty when applicable,
- the reason the project received its scope/weight.
