How Your Overall Score Works

Overall Score = 60% Project Execution + 25% Daily Reports + 15% Project Activity
60%Project Execution
Progress against time available, deadline health, completion, blockers and regular project updates.
25%Daily Reports
One off day is automatically allowed each week. One generated report counts per date. Extra clicks are logged, but do not give extra points.
15%Project Activity
Progress, status and blocker changes receive full credit. Name, due-date or Current/Future edits receive small credit. Removing or cancelling gives no credit.
